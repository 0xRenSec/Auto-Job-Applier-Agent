# Candidate profile — source material for cover letters & LLM answers

Copy this to `data/profile.md` and replace everything with your own facts. The
LLM is told to answer screening questions and write cover letters using ONLY
what's written here — so be accurate and specific. If a fact isn't here, the bot
declines rather than inventing it.

Name: Jane Doe
Location: Remote (open to EU / worldwide)
Email: jane.doe@example.com
Phone: +1 555 123 4567
LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/jane-doe

## Headline
One or two sentences: your role, years of experience, and core specialism.
e.g. "Application Security engineer with 6+ years across secure SDLC,
DevSecOps, threat modelling, and SAST/DAST/SCA tooling."

## What I do / value proposition
- Bullet the concrete things you deliver.
- Keep them truthful — these become claims in applications.

## Experience (most recent first)
- Job Title, Company (Location, years): what you owned and the tools you used.
- Job Title, Company (Location, years): ...

## Skills
List the skills, tools, languages, and platforms you can credibly claim.

## Languages
English: full professional proficiency. (List others honestly; answer "No" to
fluency questions for languages not listed here.)

## Education & certifications
- Degree, institution.
- Certifications.

## Compensation expectations
- State your salary expectation, or "Negotiable". The bot answers salary
  questions in the currency/period the form asks for, anchored to what you put
  in config.yaml → answers.salary (or the text answers).

## Tone for letters
Confident, concise, specific. Lead with the most relevant experience. No
clichés. 200-300 words.
